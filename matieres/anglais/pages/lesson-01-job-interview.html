1️⃣ Speaking — What is a job interview?
A job interview is a formal meeting between the job applicant and the employer (or interviewer) to discuss the applicant's qualifications, skills and experiences for the job.

Who is who: applicant/candidate = seeks the job · employer/boss = offers it · employee/worker = hired.
