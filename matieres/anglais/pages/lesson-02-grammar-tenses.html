2️⃣ The 4 BAC traps
Trap 1 — Past vs Perfect: dated (yesterday, in 2020) → past simple. No date + link (just, already) → present-perfect. ❌ have visited yesterday → ✅ visited yesterday.
Trap 2 — WILL vs GOING TO: visible clue (clouds!) → going to. Promise / instant choice → will.
Trap 3 — do/does/did + base verb: Did she go? · He/She/It + s: She plays.
Trap 4 — Past perfect + golden rule: 1st action → had + V3. Continuous (long) + when + simple (short). While + continuous, continuous.
