N°ElementContent

Couples: Dear Sir/Madam → Yours faithfully · Dear Mr/Ms + name → Yours sincerely. Formal tone, no contractions.

3️⃣ Example — job application (holiday computer assistant)

45 Rue Joffre, Akwa
Douala, Cameroon
daniel.mba@email.com · +237 6 00 00 00 00

19th September 2026

The Human Resources Manager
Ndongo Business Services
Bonanjo, Douala, Cameroon

Re: Application for the post of Holiday Computer Assistant

Dear Sir/Madam,

I am writing to apply for the post of Holiday Computer Assistant advertised on your notice board on 12th September 2026. I am a final-year science student in Terminale C at Lycée Joss in Douala, and I am available full-time from 1st July to 31st August.

I am punctual and trustworthy, with solid computer skills: Word and Excel for office work, and the basics of programming in C and Python. Last term I ranked 3rd in my class with an average of 15.2/20, including 17/20 in computer science. I learn fast and I work well in a team, even under pressure.

I would be grateful for the opportunity to discuss my application at an interview. I am available at your convenience and I have enclosed my school report for your consideration. Thank you for your time. I look forward to hearing from you.

Yours faithfully,

Daniel Mba
DANIEL MBA

Enclosures: school report.
