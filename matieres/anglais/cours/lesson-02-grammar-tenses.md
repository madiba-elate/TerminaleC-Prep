## 🎯 L'essentiel en 5 lignes

1. **7 temps à maîtriser** : present, past, future simple · present-perfect, past perfect · present, past continuous.
2. **Méthode** : repère le **signal word** (yesterday → past, just/already → perfect, now → continuous) puis applique la **structure**.
3. **Piège n°1** : past simple (action datée, finie) vs present-perfect (lien avec le présent, jamais daté).
4. **Piège n°2** : `did + base verbale` (jamais -ed), `he/she/it + s`, `had + V3` avant un past simple.
5. **Irréguliers** : sans les V2/V3 (went, gone, had, done…), aucun temps composé n'est possible.

## 📖 Cours complet

### 1. Les 7 temps — emploi, signaux, structures

| Temps | Emploi | Signaux | + | − | ? |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Present simple** | habits, truths, timetables | always, usually, every day | She **plays** | She **doesn't play** | **Does** she **play**? |
| **Past simple** | finished action, dated | yesterday, last week, in 2020, ago | I **visited** | I **didn't visit** | **Did** you **visit**? |
| **Future simple** | prediction, decision, promise (WILL) / plan, evidence (GOING TO) | tomorrow, soon, I think | I **will help** / am **going to study** | I **won't help** | **Will** you **help**? |
| **Present-perfect** | past→present result, experience, since/for | just, already, yet, ever, never, since, for | I **have finished** | I **haven't finished** | **Have** you **finished**? |
| **Past perfect** | action BEFORE another past action | before, after, when, by the time | I **had finished** before he arrived | I **hadn't finished** | **Had** you **finished**? |
| **Present continuous** | happening NOW, temporary, arrangement | now, at the moment, Look! | I **am studying** | I **am not studying** | **Are** you **studying**? |
| **Past continuous** | in progress at a past moment; long action cut by short one | while, when, at 8pm yesterday | I **was sleeping** when he arrived | I **wasn't sleeping** | **Were** you **sleeping**? |

### 2. Les 4 pièges du BAC

1. **Past vs Present-perfect** : date/marqueur passé (yesterday, in 2020, ago) → **past simple**.
   Jamais de date + lien présent (just, already, ever) → **present-perfect**.
   ❌ ~~I have visited London yesterday~~ → ✅ I **visited** London yesterday.
2. **WILL vs GOING TO** : prédiction sans preuve / décision instantanée / promesse → **will** ;
   projet ou prédiction avec indice → **be going to**. (« Look at those clouds! It **is going to** rain. »)
3. **Do/does/did + base verbale** : **Did** she **go** ? (jamais ~~did went~~).
   **He/she/it + s** au présent : She **plays** (jamais ~~she play~~).
4. **Past perfect = le « passé du passé »** : première action → had + V3, seconde → past simple.
   + Golden rule : **Past continuous (long) + when + past simple (short)** ;
   **While + continuous, continuous**.

### 3. Verbes irréguliers essentiels (V1 – V2 – V3)

be–was/were–been · have–had–had · do–did–done · go–went–gone · come–came–come ·
see–saw–seen · take–took–taken · make–made–made · write–wrote–written ·
speak–spoke–spoken · eat–ate–eaten · drink–drank–drunk · give–gave–given ·
know–knew–known · get–got–got · put–put–put · read–read–read · say–said–said ·
think–thought–thought · buy–bought–bought.
