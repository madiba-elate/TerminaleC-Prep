English Tenses — Use & Examples

Positive (+) • Negative (-) • Questions (?) — with signal words and structures

1. Present Simple

PRESENT

📌 When to use

  • Habits & routines
  • General truths / facts
  • Permanent situations
  • Timetables & programmes
Signal words: always, usually, often, sometimes, never, every day/week
Subject + base verb (+ s/es for he/she/it)
Positive (+)

I play football. / She plays football.

→ He/She/It + s

Negative (-)

I do not (don't) play. / He does not (doesn't) play.

Question (?)

Do you play? / Does she play?  → Yes, I do. / No, she doesn't.

2. Past Simple

PAST

📌 When to use

  • Finished action at a specific time in the past
  • Series of finished past actions
  • Past habits (with ago, in 2020...)
Signal words: yesterday, last week, in 2023, 2 days ago, when
Subject + verb-ed / 2nd column (irregular)
Positive (+)

I visited London yesterday. / She went to school.

Negative (-)

I did not (didn't) visit London. / She didn't go.

Question (?)

Did you visit London? / Did she go?

3. Future Simple

FUTURE

📌 When to use

  • WILL: prediction without evidence, instant decision, promise
  • BE GOING TO: intention/plan, prediction with evidence
Signal words: tomorrow, next week, soon, probably, I think
will + base verb  |  am/is/are + going to + base verb
Positive (+)

I will help you. / I am going to study Data.

Negative (-)

I will not (won't) help. / I am not going to study.

Question (?)

Will you help me? / Are you going to study?

4. Present Perfect

HAVE/HAS + V3

📌 When to use

  • Past action with result in the present
  • Experience without specific date (ever/never)
  • Action started in past, continues now (for/since)
Signal words: just, already, yet, ever, never, for, since, recently
Subject + have/has + past participle (V3)
Positive (+)

I have finished. / She has been to Paris.

Negative (-)

I have not (haven't) finished. / She hasn't been.

Question (?)

Have you finished? / Has she been to Paris?

5. Past Perfect

HAD + V3

📌 When to use

  • Action that happened BEFORE another past action
  • First action = Past Perfect, second = Past Simple
Signal words: before, after, when, by the time, already
Subject + had + past participle (V3)
Positive (+)

I had finished before he arrived.

Negative (-)

I had not (hadn't) finished before he arrived.

Question (?)

Had you finished before he arrived?

6. Present Continuous

AM/IS/ARE + ING

📌 When to use

  • Action happening NOW
  • Temporary action around now
  • Future arrangement (with time)
Signal words: now, at the moment, currently, Look! Listen!
Subject + am/is/are + verb-ing
Positive (+)

I am studying now. / She is watching TV.

Negative (-)

I am not studying. / She isn't watching.

Question (?)

Are you studying? / Is she watching?

7. Past Continuous

WAS/WERE + ING

📌 When to use

  • Action in progress at a specific time in the past
  • Long action interrupted by short action (Past Simple)
  • Two long actions at the same time (while)
Signal words: while, when, at 8pm yesterday, all day
Subject + was/were + verb-ing
Positive (+)

I was sleeping at 10pm. / I was eating when he arrived.

Negative (-)

I was not (wasn't) sleeping when you called.

Question (?)

Were you sleeping when I called? / Was she cooking?

💡

Golden Rule to Remember

Past Continuous (long action) + when + Past Simple (short action) → I was watching TV when the phone rang.
While + Past Continuous, Past Continuous → While I was studying, my brother was playing.
