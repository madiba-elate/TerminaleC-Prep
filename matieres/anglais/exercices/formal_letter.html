Structure of a Formal Letter

Visual Schema with Annotated Example — Homework English

📋 9 Key Parts

1

Sender's Address

Your address, top right. No name.

2

Date

Below sender's address. Ex: 12th September 2026

3

Recipient's Address

Address of the person/company, on the left.

4

Subject Line

Re: Purpose of the letter. Short and clear.

5

Salutation

Dear Sir/Madam, or Dear Mr Smith,

6

Introduction

First paragraph: reason for writing.

7

Body (2-3 paragraphs)

Details, arguments, information.

8

Conclusion

Last paragraph: what you expect / call to action.

9

Complimentary Close & Signature

Yours faithfully / sincerely + signature + full name

💡 Rule:
Yours faithfully → when you wrote Dear Sir/Madam
Yours sincerely → when you wrote Dear Mr/Ms + Name

6 — Introduction — Purpose

I am writing to apply for the Summer Internship Programme advertised on your website on 5th September 2026. I am currently a final-year student in English Studies at the Sorbonne University in Paris, and I am very interested in joining your prestigious institution.

7 — Body — Details & Arguments

I have a strong academic background and excellent communication skills in both French and English (IELTS 8.0). Last summer, I completed a one-month internship at the French Embassy in London, where I developed my organisational and teamwork skills.

Furthermore, I am highly motivated, well-organised and able to work under pressure. I am particularly interested in intercultural communication, which I believe is at the heart of your organisation's mission. I have enclosed my CV and a letter of recommendation from my professor for your consideration.

8 — Conclusion — Call to Action

I would be grateful for the opportunity to discuss my application further at an interview. I am available at your convenience and can be reached by email or phone. Thank you for considering my application. I look forward to hearing from you.

9 — Complimentary Close & Signature

Yours faithfully,

Marie Dupont
MARIE DUPONT

Enclosures: CV, Letter of Recommendation  |  Formal, polite tone • No contractions (I am, not I'm) • Clear paragraphs
