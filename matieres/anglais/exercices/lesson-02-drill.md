# ✍️ Drill — Lesson: Grammar Review (Tenses)

> **Matière :** English · Sans fiche pour la partie 1 (puis auto-corrige).

## Énoncé

**A) Put the verb in the correct tense (10 pts) :**
1. I ___ (visit) London yesterday.
2. She ___ (finish) just her homework.
3. Look at those clouds! It ___ (rain).
4. I ___ (sleep) at 10pm yesterday.
5. While I ___ (study), my brother ___ (play).
6. I ___ (never, be) to Paris.
7. He ___ (leave) before I ___ (arrive).
8. Listen! She ___ (sing).
9. She ___ (play) tennis every Sunday.
10. I think I ___ (help) you tomorrow.

**B) Transformations (5 pts) :** mets chaque phrase aux 3 formes (+/−/?) :
11. She plays tennis. 12. They visited London. 13. I am working.

---

## ✅ Corrigé

**A)** 1. **visited** (yesterday → past) · 2. **has finished** (just → perfect) ·
3. **is going to rain** (indice visible) · 4. **was sleeping** (moment passé précis) ·
5. **was studying / was playing** (while + continuous) ·
6. **have never been** (expérience, ever implicite) ·
7. **had left / arrived** (past perfect = 1re action) ·
8. **is singing** (Listen! → now) · 9. **plays** (every Sunday + she → +s) ·
10. **will help** (décision instantanée).

**B)** 11. She plays / doesn't play / Does she play? ·
12. They visited / didn't visit / Did they visit? ·
13. I am working / am not working / Are you working?

> ⭐ Réflexes notés : signal → temps → structure · daté = past ·
> did + BV · he/she/it + s · had + V3 en premier.
