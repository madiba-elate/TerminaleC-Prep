# ✍️ Homework — Lesson: Job Interview

> **Matière :** English · Sans dictionnaire pour la partie 1 (puis vérifie).

## Énoncé

**1)** Write down the **structure of a formal letter**.

**2)** Find out about the following **tenses** — give the **use** and **examples**
in the **positive, negative and question** forms :
present tense · past tense · future tense · present-perfect · past perfect ·
present and past continuous.

---

## ✅ Corrigé type

### Question 1 — Structure of a formal letter (9 parties)

1. Sender's address (top right, no name) — 2. Date — 3. Recipient's address (left) —
4. Subject line (`Re: …`) — 5. Salutation (`Dear Sir/Madam,` ou `Dear Mr/Ms + name,`) —
6. Introduction (purpose) — 7. Body, 2-3 paragraphs (details, arguments) —
8. Conclusion (call to action) — 9. Complimentary close + signature
(`Yours faithfully` après Sir/Madam · `Yours sincerely` après un nom).

Barème indicatif : 9 parties nommées dans l'ordre (3 pts) + 1 exemple de formule
de politesse adaptée (1 pt).

### Question 2 — Tenses (use + 3 forms each)

| Tense | Use | + | − | ? |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Present | habits, truths | She **plays** | She **doesn't play** | **Does** she **play**? |
| Past | finished past action | I **visited** | I **didn't visit** | **Did** you **visit**? |
| Future | prediction, decision, promise | I **will help** | I **won't help** | **Will** you **help**? |
| Present-perfect | past action with present result | I **have finished** | I **haven't finished** | **Have** you **finished**? |
| Past perfect | action before another past action | I **had left** when… | I **hadn't left** | **Had** you **left**? |
| Present continuous | action happening now | I **am working** | I **am not working** | **Are** you **working**? |
| Past continuous | interrupted past action | I **was sleeping** when… | I **wasn't sleeping** | **Were** you **sleeping**? |

→ Fiche complète : `matieres/anglais/exercices/english_tenses.html`.

Barème indicatif : 1 pt par temps (use + 3 formes correctes).

> ⭐ Phrases qui rapportent : « Dear Sir/Madam → Yours faithfully » /
> « did + base verbale, jamais -ed » / « he/she/it + s au présent ».
